Experiment E2, version 2: does Luke's selection alone produce the Q/M style difference?
permutations 5000, bootstrap 2000, classifier permutations 1000, seed 20261116
Version 2 applies a written revision of the design made AFTER version 1 stopped at its power gate and
BEFORE any of the ten comparisons had been computed (version 1 output: research_stylometry_v1.txt).
Changes: (A) selection classes by shared words per verse, plus M*/L*; (B) power gate = M vs L sayings;
(C) bias-corrected bootstrap interval for E; (D) E(1)-E(2), E(5)-E(6) with paired-bootstrap intervals;
(E) classifier descriptive only, "too few chunks" under 6 chunks in a class.
